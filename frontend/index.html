❓ D.E.L.P.H.I. — FAQ & tutorial

🚀 Getting started — a two-minute tour
  1. Home is your situational overview: columns curated by D.E.L.P.H.I. (top events, breaking, conflict, politics, business, sci-tech, social pulse), generated live from everything ingested. Scroll the board sideways; each column scrolls its own articles vertically.
  2. Click any event card or article row to open Event Focus — synopsis, map, cross-source timeline, outlets (each links to its article), and related events. Every story is an event, even single-source ones. Viewed events dim so you can see what you've already triaged.
  3. Press 📌 on a Home column to copy it into 📋 My feeds, or + Create to build your own with the four-step wizard: Topic (keywords/boolean queries), Where (countries or a shape drawn on the map), Refine (categories, platforms, importance…), Review (summary + live match count).
  4. The 📋 Feed / 🔔 Alert toggle at the top of the wizard decides what you're making: a feed is a column you read, an alert notifies you live. Create an alert for anything you must know about the moment it happens.
  5. Add sources or track new topics in Sources; tune appearance, language, timestamps, and notifications in ⚙ Settings.
📋 Feeds vs 🔔 Alerts — what's the difference?

Both use the same criteria (keywords, boolean queries, countries, categories, map areas, importance…). The difference is posture:

A feed is a place you look. It's a column on your board that always shows the current matching articles, sorted and optionally grouped into events. Nothing interrupts you.

An alert looks for you. Every ingest cycle (about every 5 minutes), new articles are tested against your active alerts. A hit is pushed live to the dashboard — toast, sound, bell counter, optional desktop notification — and stored in the alert's history with a map of where hits happened.

Rule of thumb: broad topics you check on your schedule → feed. Narrow, time-critical triggers ("anything Critical inside this drawn box") → alert.

Convert any time. Both are built in the same wizard — pick with the 📋 Feed / 🔔 Alert toggle at the top. Open an existing feed or alert with ✎ and flip that toggle to turn one into the other; every criterion carries over, and Save completes the conversion.

📊 How importance scoring works

Every article gets a transparent 0–100 score — no black box. It adds up:

  • Source reach — base score by scope: international 45, national 35, local 25; major wires (tier 1) +8, niche/local outlets (tier 3) −4.
  • Breaking signals — weighted terms in the headline/summary ("breaking", "earthquake", "state of emergency", "assassination"…), capped at +30.
  • Geographic breadth — a story implicating two or more countries +6.
  • Corroboration — +8 for each additional outlet reporting a similar headline within 48h, capped at +24. Many independent outlets = the story matters.

Tiers: ▲ Critical ≥ 80 · ◆ High 60–79 · ● Notable 40–59 · ○ Routine < 40. An event's importance is its highest-scoring report. Filter any feed or alert with the minimum-importance slider.

🧵 Events, Event Focus, and dimming

Coverage of the same story from different outlets is clustered into an event (headline similarity, 72-hour rolling window). Feeds with "group into events" show one card per story with a report/source count.

Selecting any story — grouped card or single article row — opens Event Focus: the founding report's synopsis, stats, a map of tagged places, the full timeline newest-first, "covered by" outlet links, and related events. The article links live inside the panel (timeline and outlet chips); alert hit lists link straight to articles for speed. Once viewed, the event dims everywhere it appears (hover to read at full strength). Views are remembered on your account, on every device.

Staleness: a feed can enable 🕰 "Hide events with no recent updates" (wizard, Review step); the threshold is yours, in ⚙ Settings. Hiding is display-only — clustering keeps attaching new similar reports to hidden events, so an event that gets an update reappears immediately with its complete timeline. Feeds can also be pinned to an explicit date range in the wizard's Refine step.

🔍 Boolean queries — syntax and the builder

Queries match the article's headline, summary, and fetched body text. Syntax: AND, OR, NOT, parentheses, "exact phrases". Google-style also works: -term for NOT, | for OR, smart quotes are cleaned up.

("supply chain" OR semiconductor) AND (china OR taiwan) NOT rumor

A feed can hold several queries — each runs separately, all fill the same feed. Prefer boxes over syntax? Click 🪄 build next to any query row: "must include at least one from each row, none of these", with a live match count.

🌍 Where the news comes from

A catalog of 85+ sources across every continent — international wires, national outlets, local papers — plus the social platforms with open feeds: Reddit, Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube (X/Facebook/Instagram expose no open feeds). Feeds/alerts can filter by platform.

Saving a feed or alert with queries/keywords also creates a Google News tracker (the 🔍 checkbox) that ingests worldwide press coverage of your topic, so matches aren't limited to the catalog. Track topics manually in Sources (📰 Press / 💬 Social), add any RSS/Atom URL, edit sources in place, and watch their health dots: green = last poll OK, red = the error is in the tooltip.

For matching, each new article's page is fetched once and reduced to text, so criteria see the whole story, not just the headline.

🗺 Drawing areas on the map

In the wizard's "Where" step, draw a polygon, rectangle, or circle. Only news geolocated inside the shape matches — via places mentioned in the article (countries and major cities), falling back to the country of the outlet. Works identically for alerts: "alert me on anything Critical inside this box" is the classic use.

🌐 Translation

Pick a reading language in ⚙ Settings (defaults to your browser's). Articles in other languages are machine-translated on the fly and marked "🌐 translated from XX"; translations are cached so each article is translated once. Choose "Original languages" to turn it off.

🔐 Accounts, privacy, and what's shared

Private to your account: feeds, alerts and their history, viewed-event dimming. Sign in with username or email from any device.

Shared by everyone on this server: the article database and the source catalog — one ingestion pipeline feeds all users, and any user can add or edit sources. Display preferences (theme, timestamps, sound) stay on the device.

🆕 Staying current — automatic FAQ and the What's-new popup

This FAQ opens by itself on your first visit, and again whenever you return after a week or more away — a quick refresher costs nothing.

If anything shipped while you were gone, a 🆕 What's new popup lists every update you missed, grouped by date (miss three updates, see all three). Delphi tracks this per account from when you last opened the app, so it works across devices.

Both are always available on demand in ⚙ Settings → Help.

🛠 Troubleshooting

A feed is empty: open it (✎) and use Preview matches while removing one filter at a time — the culprit is usually an over-tight combination. For niche topics make sure the 🔍 worldwide-coverage box is ticked, then hit ⟳ and give it a cycle or two.

⟳ says a poll is already running: the scheduled 5-minute cycle is mid-flight; results land when it finishes.

A source shows a red dot: hover it in Sources for the exact error. 404 = moved/dead URL (edit it in place), 429 = rate-limited (backs off automatically), "not a feed" = the URL returns a web page, not RSS.

Stat tile shows …/N: the first poll since startup hasn't finished.
